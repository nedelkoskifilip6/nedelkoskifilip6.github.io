Photography
& provenance.

Independent Portfolio Concept. Not affiliated with Miku Vancouver. The visual identity, design and frontend implementation are an independent study by Filip Nedelkoski. No partnership, employment, endorsement or commission is implied.

The Hero photograph was supplied by the project owner; its desktop background was extended with AI. The remaining editorial photography is licensed from Unsplash. All images are hosted locally. Food images illustrate Japanese cuisine and do not depict the named Miku dishes. Dining and craft photographs are independent atmosphere studies photographed at other venues, rather than at Miku Vancouver. The waterfront photograph is a Coal Harbour location study, rather than the view from the restaurant.

Type & technology

Original typographic brand treatment. Cormorant Garamond and DM Sans are self-hosted through Fontsource under the SIL Open Font License. Built with Astro, TypeScript, React, Tailwind CSS, GSAP and ScrollTrigger. Booking continues with Miku’s official OpenTable provider.

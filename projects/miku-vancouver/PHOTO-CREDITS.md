## Art direction

`hero.jpg`: the strongest landscape hero candidate. A diagonal black nigiri platter, natural seafood colors, restrained side lighting, and substantial dark negative space. Center focus works on mobile; avoid stretching. `sushi.jpg`: use as a close-up for the Aburi technique story, with its illustrative caption. `sashimi.jpg`: a cinematic alternate with salmon in matte-black ceramic bowls. `chef.jpg`: black-and-white sushi chef preparation, useful for a quieter editorial interlude. `interior.jpg`: Kyoto Japanese dining study; keep its location caption visible.

Rejected authentic Miku Flickr photographs carried noncommercial/no-derivatives restrictions, which were unsuitable for a reusable frontend portfolio with image crops. Miku official-site photographs were not reused without a license or explicit permission.

An independent point of view.

This website is an independent front-end development portfolio concept inspired by exceptional architecture and the feeling of discovering a hidden place. It is not affiliated with, commissioned by, or endorsed by Sotheby’s International Realty.

“The extraordinary, unseen.” and all editorial headlines are creative concept copy. They are not presented as official brand slogans or communications.

The Edge of Silence, The Forest Hideaway, and The Silent Form are conceptual architectural studies. Their names, narratives and atmosphere labels are imaginative, rather than verified property information. Photographs shown together can depict different places or architectural visualizations. No address, price, specification or availability is asserted. No property is offered for sale.

The photograph comes first.

The opening restores the previously reviewed AI-assisted cleanup of an owner-supplied aerial photograph of a red-brick estate, formal walled gardens, fountains, a swimming pool, a tennis court and mature trees. The 1280 × 853 pixel input was inspected and used directly as the edit target. Its temporary original attachment later expired; an untouched original is not retained. The selected master is identified as an AI derivative. Photographer attribution and original usage provenance were not supplied.

The restored output has native dimensions of 1536 × 1024 pixels and is delivered without post-generation enlargement as lossless WebP, with a 1024 × 683 pixel responsive derivative. No new AI processing was used for this restoration. The cleanup aims to retain the daylight palette, aerial perspective and estate layout while reducing noise and compression artifacts. Fine detail is model-reconstructed; perfect source fidelity or factual detail recovery is not claimed.

Desktop and mobile share this estate scene. The edge-to-edge opening remains 100svh with top-oriented cover rendering, keeping the main residence visible while viewport shapes crop the outer grounds differently. The rounded entrance transition is aligned to facade windows. The approved soft black shadows behind Hero text remain in place. Replaced courtyard and earlier apartment Hero assets are retired outside the application and are not deployed or reused elsewhere.

A considered experience.

Architecture sets the rhythm. The architectural entrance, architectural light reveal, lateral destination journey and spatial gallery share the same scroll choreography on desktop and mobile. Typography and composition adapt to the viewport; reduced-motion preferences receive a quieter presentation. The interface supports touch, mouse and keyboard and uses Astro, TypeScript and GSAP, with locally hosted photographs and typography.
